## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-09

First public release.

### Added
- Sign-in with a Canvas personal access token, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
- Dashboard, course pages (announcements, assignments, discussions, grades, modules, pages, files,
  syllabus, people, quizzes) and external tools in a signed-in web view.
- Assignment submission: text, URL and file uploads.
- Calendar, To Do and Inbox.
- Google Calendar sync of due dates and events, with reminders.
- Notifications for announcements, messages, grades, discussions and due dates, checked every 15 minutes.
- Offline copies of chosen courses, including files, kept up to date on Wi-Fi.
- Light, dark and Material You themes.
