# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, and accept constructive criticism
gracefully. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information
are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through GitHub, by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Rafat-Aman/canvos/security/advisories/new) or contacting
[@Rafat-Aman](https://github.com/Rafat-Aman). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may
ban contributors temporarily or permanently.
